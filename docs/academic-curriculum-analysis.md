- **Crediti**: Corso universitario integrato
- **Focus**: Principi blockchain, Bitcoin, smart contracts, applicazioni decentralizzate
- **Metodo**: Lezioni frontali + progetto pratico
- **Link**: [UniBo Course 2024/2025](https://www.unibo.it/en/study/phd-professional-masters-specialisation-schools-and-other-programmes/course-unit-catalogue/course-unit/2024/461739)

**Learning Outcomes**:
- Conoscenza temi rilevanti: blockchain, criptovalute, smart contracts
- Capacità di sviluppare smart contracts semplici deployabili su blockchain

**Contenuti Chiave**:
- Principi concettuali e fondamenti della blockchain
- Rete Bitcoin e sue implementazioni
- Distributed ledger alternatives
- Building blocks legati a ricerca in computer science

### 5. **Stanford Online - CS251: Cryptocurrencies and Blockchain Technologies**
- **Livello**: Advanced undergraduate / Graduate
- **Focus**: Aspetti tecnici, sicurezza software, distributed consensus
- **Link**: [Stanford CS251](https://cs251.stanford.edu/syllabus.html)

**Syllabus Dettagliato** (20 lezioni):

#### **Bitcoin Mechanics** (Lectures 1-3)
- Intro to cryptography & cryptocurrencies
- Merkle trees
- Bitcoin nuts and bolts (Block Chain, Transactions, P2P Network)
- Wallets: managing and protecting crypto assets

#### **Consensus Protocols** (Lectures 4-6)
- Classical consensus: network models, SMR (State Machine Replication)
- Synchrony, Asynchrony, Partial Synchrony
- Threshold adversary, Dolev-Strong Authenticated Broadcast
- Nakamoto consensus, sybil resistance
- Proof-of-stake, accountability, incentives
- Blockchain selfish mining

#### **Ethereum & DApps** (Lectures 7-8)
- Ethereum: Decentralized Apps, EVM, blockchain
- Programming in Solidity
- EVM opcodes and gas costs

#### **DeFi & Economics** (Lectures 9-12)
- Stablecoins and lending protocols (Compound)
- Decentralized exchanges (Uniswap, YieldSpace)
- Maximal Extractable Value (MEV)
- Legal aspects and regulation (Howey Test)

#### **Other Layer-1** (Lecture 13)
- Solana, Sui, Aptos architectures
- AlpenGlow, Solana programming model

#### **Privacy** (Lectures 14-16)
- De-anonymizing blockchain and mixing
- zk-SNARKs for privacy
- Zcash, Confidential Transactions
- PLONK SNARK, Polynomial commitments

#### **Scaling** (Lectures 17-18)
- Payment channels and state channels
- Lightning Network
- Rollups: Optimistic, ZK, based, native
- Arbitrum, Blobs on Ethereum

#### **Advanced Topics** (Lectures 19-20)
- Account abstraction
- Bridging (Celo Optics)
- Post-quantum blockchains
- AI and blockchains
- Private DAOs

## 📊 Macro-Aree Comuni (Solidità Accademica)

### **1. FONDAMENTI TECNICI**
- Crittografia applicata (hash, merkle trees, firme digitali)
- Architettura blockchain (UTXO vs account model)
- Meccanismi di consenso (PoW, PoS, BFT)
- P2P networks e distributed systems
- Smart contracts e EVM

### **2. BITCOIN & LAYER-1**
- Bitcoin protocol deep dive
- Lightning Network e payment channels
- Alternative layer-1 (Ethereum, Solana, etc.)
- Scalability solutions (rollups, state channels)

### **3. ETHEREUM & SMART CONTRACTS**
- Solidity programming
- Gas optimization
- DApp development
- Security best practices

### **4. DEFI & TOKENOMICS**
- Stablecoins (algorithmic, collateralized)
- Lending protocols (Compound, Aave)
- DEX (AMM, order books)
- Yield farming e liquidity mining
- MEV (Maximal Extractable Value)

### **5. PRIVACY & SECURITY**
- De-anonymization techniques
- Mixing protocols
- zk-SNARKs e zero-knowledge proofs
- Confidential transactions
- Wallet security e key management

### **6. WEB3 & APPLICAZIONI**
- NFTs (standards, use cases)
- DAOs (governance, voting)
- Digital identity
- Metaverse foundations
- Decentralized social media

### **7. ECONOMIA & REGOLAMENTAZIONE**
- Crypto economics
- Market dynamics
- Fiscal aspects (taxation)

- International regulation

### **8. SCALABILITÀ & FUTURO**
- Layer-2 solutions
- Cross-chain bridges
- Post-quantum cryptography
- AI + blockchain convergence
- Interoperability protocols

## 🎯 Raccomandazioni per Tradelia

1. **Bitcoin Fundamentals**: UTXO model, mining, consensus
2. **Ethereum & Smart Contracts**: EVM, Solidity basics, gas

3. **MEV**: Front-running, sandwich attacks

2. **Post-Quantum**: Quantum-resistant algorithms

4. **AI + Blockchain**: Emerging convergence
