## 🎨 CARD DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

### Minimum Information on Card
1. **Firm Logo** (custom SVG, not emoji)
2. **Challenge Type** (Evaluation, Express, Stellar, etc.)
3. **Account Size** ($5,000, $10,000, etc.)
4. **Entry Fee** ($19, $29, etc.)
5. **Refundable Badge** (if applicable)
6. **Challenge Steps** (1-Step, 2-Step, 3-Step)
7. **Profit Target** (6%, 8%, 10%)
8. **Max Drawdown** (6%, 8%, 10%)
9. **Profit Split** (80%, 90%, 99%)
10. **Key Differentiator** (Cheapest, Best Scaling, Instant, etc.)

